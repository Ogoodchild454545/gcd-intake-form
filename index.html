Data protection & privacy

The information you provide is used to assess, plan and deliver your dietetic care at The GCD Clinic. It is held securely, and access is limited to the dietitian responsible for your care.

Your form is submitted through a third-party service acting on our instructions. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing. It will not be shared with anyone else without your agreement, unless required by law or necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

As you're filling this in for someone else, we'll keep your contact details with theirs so we can talk to you about their care. The dietitian will also check consent with you both at the first appointment.
